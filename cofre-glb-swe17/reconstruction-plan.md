# Reconstruction Plan: Crown Chest (procedural from GLB)

## Source of truth
- GLB: `cofre.glb` (single triangulated mesh, ~318k vertices, 500k triangles, one material with baseColor/RM/normal maps).
- Reference visual: `crown-chest.png` (validates colors, materials, gloss, emblem glow).

## Inferred from GLB
- Single mesh generated by `tripo_*`; no authored hierarchy, pivots, or named parts. All component breakdown is inferred from spatial + color analysis.
- Bounding box: W≈1.0, H≈0.783, D≈0.747.
- Body/lid split at Y≈0.548.
- Symmetry: bilateral about X=0 and Z=0 (except the front emblem and rear hinges; both are mirrored placement-wise).
- Components are inferred by color zones and face normals from the baked textures.

## Component hierarchy (runtime)

```
root
├── bodyPivot
│   └── body (main base box)
├── lidPivot
│   └── lidGroup
│       └── lid (main lid box)
├── reinforcementsPivot
│   └── verticalBands[8], horizontalBands[8]
├── cornersPivot
│   └── cornerGuards[8]
├── hingesPivot
│   └── hingeCylinders[2 or 3]
├── handlesPivot
│   └── handles[2]
└── emblemPivot
    └── crownEmblem
```

## Geometry decisions derived from GLB

### Body
- Type: `BoxGeometry` / `RoundedBoxGeometry`.
- Dimensions: width 1.00, height 0.55, depth 0.75.
- Local Y center: 0.275 (sits on Y=0).
- Rounding: soft edge bevel ~0.02 on vertical edges (metal trim hides exact seam).

### Lid
- Type: `BoxGeometry` / `RoundedBoxGeometry`.
- Dimensions: width 0.99, height 0.235, depth 0.75.
- Pivot: `lidPivot` located at (0, 0.548, -0.373), the rear lower edge.
- Lid local offset: (0, 0.1175, 0.373) so its rear-bottom edge aligns with the pivot.
- Rounding: top edge chamfer ~0.03.

### Reinforcements (metal bands)
- Thin boxes along the 12 edges of body + lid.
- Size: thickness 0.02, protrusion ~0.005 beyond the shell.
- 4 vertical corner posts (full height 0.783).
- Horizontal band around the body-lid seam (perimeter).
- Top rim band on lid.
- Bottom rim band on body.

### Corners
- Larger chamfered cubes / octagonal prisms at each of the 8 vertices.
- Size: 0.07 x 0.07 x 0.07, with a 45-degree chamfer to form the gold corner guards visible in the reference.

### Hinges
- Cylinders along the rear seam, spanning most of the lid width.
- Diameter ~0.04, length ~0.65, centered on Z=-0.373 at Y≈0.56.

### Handles
- Pull handles on the left (-X) and right (+X) side faces.
- U-shaped `TubeGeometry` or `TorusGeometry` segment, mounted around the side center.

### Emblem
- Crown shape on the front face of the body, centered on X=0, Y≈0.30, Z≈0.36.
- `ExtrudeGeometry` from a 2D `Shape` or a raised rounded box with a crown profile.
- Slight self-illumination to mimic the glowing emblem in the reference.

## Materials

### bodyMaterial
- Class: glossy painted / teal-blue.
- Color: `#0B3C5A` (deep teal), with a subtle blue-green gradient from the image.
- MeshPhysicalMaterial:
  - `roughness: 0.18`
  - `metalness: 0.10`
  - `clearcoat: 0.40`
  - `clearcoatRoughness: 0.20`

### lidMaterial
- Class: glossy purple.
- Color: `#5D18A0`.
- MeshPhysicalMaterial:
  - `roughness: 0.15`
  - `metalness: 0.10`
  - `clearcoat: 0.45`
  - `clearcoatRoughness: 0.20`

### goldMaterial
- Class: polished gold trim.
- Color: `#FFC107`.
- MeshStandardMaterial / MeshPhysicalMaterial:
  - `roughness: 0.12`
  - `metalness: 1.0`
  - `clearcoat: 0.60`

### emblemMaterial
- Base: goldMaterial.
- Adds `emissive: #FFC107`, `emissiveIntensity: 0.4`.

## Runtime pivots
- `bodyPivot` at the origin; body geometry and all static trim attach here.
- `lidPivot` at (0, 0.548, -0.373); opening rotation around X by +90..+110 degrees.

## Review plan
- Render a front view comparable to the reference image.
- Compare: silhouette, proportions, color zones, gloss, emblem glow, metal trim placement.
- Iteration: adjust material colors/roughness/metalness and geometry proportions until the visual match is acceptable.

## Known approximations
- The GLB is one continuous triangulated surface; the procedural decomposition is an approximation of its visual form.
- Exact crown silhouette and handle curvature are inferred from the image because the GLB stores them only as pixel/texture data.
- The interior of the chest is not modeled; the lid opens as a single rigid shell.
