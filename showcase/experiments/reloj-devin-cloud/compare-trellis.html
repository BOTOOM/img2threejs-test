TRELLIS GLB — baselineMesh produced by the TRELLIS image-to-3D model, not by this pipeline. Used only as a render baseline for comparison; none of its topology or materials were copied.

The middle panel is generated code: Three.js primitives assembled from a sculpt spec, with semantic part names and procedural PBR materials. The right panel is the TRELLIS mesh (trellis-community/TRELLIS), a single untextured-hierarchy triangle soup — more organic in the central relief, but with no semantic structure. TRELLIS leaves metallicFactor at 1.0, which renders its baked albedo almost black, so this viewer neutralises metalness and computes vertex normals for a fair silhouette comparison. Both panels use the same camera fit, so the azimuth selector points both at the same angle.
