TRELLIS GLB — baselineMesh produced by the TRELLIS image-to-3D model, not by this pipeline. Used only as a render baseline for comparison; none of its topology or materials were copied.

Each cat was treated as an independent subject with its own sculpt spec and gate cycle. The middle panel is generated code: Three.js primitives (capsules, lathes, tubes, curve sweeps) with procedural canvas-generated PBR textures — the demonstrated ceiling for organic subjects is a stylised, recognisable figure, not photorealism. The right panel is the TRELLIS mesh (trellis-community/TRELLIS), used only as a render baseline. TRELLIS leaves metallicFactor at 1.0, which renders its baked albedo almost black, so this viewer neutralises metalness for a fair comparison. Both 3D panels use the same camera fit, so the azimuth selector points both at the same angle. Toggle Neutral shading to strip texture maps and compare silhouettes only.
